THE FETUS LAUNCHER — the user's own model, kept for later (at the user's
request: "make this gun available for later"). Not in the game yet: it
lives here, under tools/ (which Godot does not import), so it costs the
builds nothing until it is made a weapon — then it goes to
assets/models/ as the other guns did (weapon3d.gd GUNS).

    fetus_launcher.glb           as it came (Blender glTF I/O 5.2.40),
                                 47.6 MB; 7z'd in two volumes
    fetus_launcher_preview.png   four views of it (godot/tests/model_shot.gd
                                 reads it here unimported)

IN THE FILE: 11 parts, one node and one material each, every material
PBR and baked (colour, normal, AO-roughness-metal; 34 PNGs, 1024 square
but the body's three at 2048), 86,325 triangles:

    main_gun_body      25,959   the body, stock to front
    barrel_ext         45,020   the red caged barrel (the cage's holes)
    barrel_mid          2,462   the barrel's sleeve
    barrel_int            270   its bore
    container_frame     4,172   the canister's frame on top
    glass                 512   the canister: KHR_materials_transmission,
                                ior 1.52 — Godot draws it opaque; give it
                                gun_glass.gdshader as the hopper's
    flesh               1,666   what is in the canister
    flesh_ball            862   the round of it
    grip_ext            1,056   the grip
    grip_int            3,108   its inside
    mechanical_thing    1,238   the works under the canister

Its own units: 30.8 long, 8.5 wide, 15.1 high, the barrel to +z (its
muzzle near z = 17.7), the stock to -z, the canister standing up over
the middle; weapon3d's `fit` scales it to the hand.

FOR THE GAME, when it is wanted: the barrel's 45k triangles are most of
it (decimate the cage, or bake it to a cut-out on a simpler sleeve, as
the launcher's remesh was), the textures down to 1024 or 512, and the
glass to gun_glass with the flesh lit behind it.
